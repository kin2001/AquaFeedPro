## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand, edit existing logo into a phone home-screen app icon.
Input image is the approved AquaFeed Pro logo; preserve the identity of its left-hand emblem.
Create a single square app launcher icon, ideally 1024 x 1024. Remove all typography and the wordmark. Use the same fish silhouette facing up and right toward two circular feed pellets, with the same single curved water wave below and the same round eye. Render the fish, pellets, and wave in solid off-white #F4F4F5 on a uniform solid graphite #18181B background. This reverses the transparent cutouts of the source into off-white forms while retaining the recognizable emblem shape.
Fill the entire square canvas with graphite, edge to edge, fully opaque. Do not draw an inset rounded-square tile, an outer frame, or round the actual canvas corners; the phone launcher will apply its own shape mask. Center the symbol group optically with comfortable safe margins: every essential part inside the central roughly 64 percent of canvas width and height so it survives circular or rounded-square launcher masking.
Crisp flat vector-like shapes, clean edges, bold enough at small phone icon sizes. Strictly two colors, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no glow, no 3D, no mockup, no phone frame, no text, no watermark. One final icon only, not a presentation sheet.
